Privacy

Terigo Privacy Policy

Terigo stores your saved routes, route notes, tags, offline route files, and synced activity data so the app can browse, prepare, and follow routes from one iPhone workflow.

What Terigo Stores

On your device, Terigo stores saved routes, route notes, tags, offline route files, synced activity data, and credentials protected by the iOS Keychain.

When you use account-backed features, Terigo's hosted service stores your Strava athlete ID, display name, profile image URL, hashed Terigo session tokens, synced lists and sharing settings, route data you choose to share, and feedback you choose to submit. The hosted service verifies your identity with Strava but does not retain your Strava access or refresh token after that request finishes.

How Terigo Uses Network Services

When you connect a Strava account, the app exchanges authorization data with Strava and the configured Strava auth broker so it can import the routes and activities you approved. If you choose to upload an activity, the app sends the selected GPX and activity data back to Strava.

When you inspect, share, follow, or download routes, the app may send coordinates or route metadata to providers that power those features, including Apple services for search and geocoding, Mapbox for map tiles, terrain, and offline regions, Supabase for account-backed list sharing, Open-Meteo for route-start weather forecasts, and Overpass/OpenStreetMap services for route surface classification.

Location and Notifications

When route tracking is active and Continuous GPS is enabled, Terigo may continue receiving location updates while the app is in the background or the screen is locked so it can keep route progress current and alert you if you go off route.

If you turn Continuous GPS off during route tracking, Terigo stops continuous background GPS and refreshes your position the next time you reopen the app. If you allow notifications, Terigo can send local off-route alerts during an active tracking session.

What Terigo Does Not Do

Terigo does not include third-party advertising, does not sell personal data, and does not use cross-app tracking.

Retention and Deletion

Terigo account sessions expire after 45 days. Account and sharing data is retained while your account exists.

To delete your hosted Terigo profile, sessions, owned lists, sharing permissions, feedback, and stored shared-route files, open Account settings in Terigo and choose Delete Terigo Account. This does not delete your Strava account. Routes stored only on your iPhone remain until you remove them or delete the app.

Support

Public support and App Review follow-up can use the support page, support email, and the Terigo issue tracker.

Hosted-service logs and operational metadata may be retained by infrastructure providers only as needed for debugging, reliability, abuse prevention, and security monitoring, subject to those providers' retention and access controls.
