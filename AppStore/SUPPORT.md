# Terigo Support

Terigo support is available for account issues, route-sync problems, App Review follow-up, and general product questions.

Support channels:

- Support page: `https://maxyaport.com/terigo/support.html`
- Support email: `yaportmax@gmail.com`
- Issue tracker: `https://github.com/yaportmax/Terigo/issues`
- Response target: `2 business days`

Include this information in support requests when possible:

- Device model
- iOS version
- Terigo version
- Whether the issue happened during Strava connect, route sync, activity sync/upload, route tracking, GPX import, list sharing, map browsing, or offline download
- The route name or activity name involved
- A screenshot or short description of what happened
