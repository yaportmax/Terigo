# Code of Conduct

Terigo contributors are expected to keep project spaces respectful, useful, and safe.

- Be considerate and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Do not harass, threaten, demean, or disclose private information.
- Respect athlete privacy. Never post another person's route, location, identity, or credentials.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security contact when a public issue would expose sensitive information.
